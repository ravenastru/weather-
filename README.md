1. The Aghora Path & Elemental Mechanics
In Aghora (the non-dual, rawest stream of Tantra), Aghor means "that which is not terrifying"—a state of zero aversion, zero attraction, and absolute neutrality (Samata).
	•	Bhuta Shuddhi (Mastery of the Elements): Aghora teaches that the outer universe (Brahmanda) and the inner body (Pinda) are identical. Before a soul can affect outer weather, it undergoes Bhuta Shuddhi—burning the seeds of karma in the internal fire (Agni) until Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Ether inside the practitioner return to their unmanifest, elemental purity (Tattvas).
	•	Smashana (The Cremation Ground Alignment): An Aghori operates in the Smashana—the place where form burns away to reveal raw elemental essence. A soul that has achieved this does not "command" the weather; their energetic field is so stripped of personal ego static that the macrocosmic elements mirror their internal state.
